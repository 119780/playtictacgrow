=======================

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

Matchmaking, reconnects, and a Play hub
- Added elo-based matchmaking with ranked and unranked queues
- Games now survive accidental disconnects — 60 seconds to reconnect
  before it counts as a forfeit, with a "Currently Playing" button to
  resume from the dashboard
- Added a Forfeit option (sidebar button + mobile menu) for deliberate exits
- Consolidated Local, Host, Join, matchmaking, and friend invites into
  one Play page
- Added a leaderboard — click your ELO stat to see the top 20
- Added keyboard shortcut hints (1/2/3) directly on the action buttons
- Added this changelog

Accounts, friends, and ranked play
- Google sign-in via Supabase, with guest play still fully supported
- Friends system: add by code, accept/decline, invite friends directly
- Ranked toggle when hosting or matchmaking
- Hidden Elo rating system feeding into your ladder rank

Core game
- Local two-player and online play (host/join by room code)
- Tic, Grow, and No actions with full rule validation, server-authoritative
- Randomized starting player each game
